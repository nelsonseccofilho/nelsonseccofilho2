### Safe factual narrative

Nelson authored and maintains the public AI-Assisted Design Engineering Operating System, a versioned framework for persistent, evidence-driven collaboration between Product Designers and AI agents. Public repository evidence is anchored to https://github.com/nelsonseccofilho/ai-assisted-design-engineering-operating-system and commit `2910154799ed28416475a359a9ea536599f38451`.

The operating model is **used as an operational baseline in real client engagements through N3LX Digital Business**, adapted to each engagement's confidentiality, evidence, governance, and tool constraints. Cross-chat/runtime continuity was exercised and validated in real operational use; live artifact reconciliation with Figma MCP was exercised in the private adopting runtime. These operational-use statements are owner-confirmed facts; public screenshots establish repository/documentation evidence and do not disclose or independently demonstrate private client operations.

The framework documents a workflow connecting stakeholder meetings, approved recording, local transcription, human evidence review, requirements/decisions, Product/UX analysis, artifact work, QA, refinement, and persistent handoff. OBS Studio and WhisperX are examples of the real local evidence-ingestion workflow, not mandatory framework dependencies. Machine transcription is evidence requiring review and must not be presented as automatic truth.

Public and private trust boundaries are intentionally separate: the public framework contains generic reusable method, while real client-specific runtime context and evidence remain private. Real screenshots prove existence. Native diagrams explain systems. Editorial representations protect confidential work.

### Publication and confidentiality boundaries

The public framework contains the generic reusable method. Real client-specific runtime context, stakeholders, evidence, artifact references, and project history remain private. Public repository screenshots prove the framework and its documentation, not confidential client operations.

Do not publish or use the following to prove framework adoption:

- Confidential client names or identifying information, unless separately authorized.
- Stakeholder identities.
- Private Figma URLs, Figma file keys, or Figma node IDs.
- Proprietary meeting recordings or proprietary transcripts.
- Private Project Runtime contents.
- Confidential product screenshots.
- Client-specific evidence or project history.

### Prohibited claims

- Do not invent productivity percentages or time-saving or efficiency metrics.
- Do not claim hosted CI PASS when the workflow did not actually execute; unavailable infrastructure is not a passed validation.
- Do not imply that every N3LX Digital Business client engagement uses an identical runtime configuration.
- Do not present editorial representations as original or confidential product UI.
- Do not treat machine transcription as automatic truth.
- Do not imply that public GitHub screenshots independently prove private client operations.

### Approved consulting wording

Preferred meaning: `used as an operational baseline in real client engagements through N3LX Digital Business`.

Avoid absolute wording such as: `used identically with every client`.
